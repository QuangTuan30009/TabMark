1. Overview & Single Purpose

This Privacy Policy describes how TabMark ("we", "our", or "us"), including the TabMark Web Application (https://tab-mark-cyan.vercel.app/) and the TabMark Browser Extension ("TabMark - Smart Bookmark Saver"), collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

Single Purpose: TabMark is designed solely to help users save, organize, categorize, and access their personal web bookmarks in a visual Kanban board structure. We are firmly committed to user privacy and data transparency.

2. Information We Collect

We strictly adhere to data minimization principles. We only process data necessary to provide bookmark management functions:

A. Bookmark Information (Explicitly Initiated by User)

  • Page URL & Title: Collected ONLY when you explicitly click "Lưu vào TabMark" (Save Bookmark) or choose "Lưu trang này vào TabMark" from the context menu.
  • User Metadata: Tags, notes, and collection selections you manually choose.
  • Favicon: The public icon of the bookmarked site to display on your dashboard.

B. Account Information

  • When you create or log into a TabMark account, your Email address and authentication tokens are managed securely via Supabase Authentication to synchronize your bookmarks across your devices.
What We NEVER Collect: We DO NOT track your browsing history, we DO NOT monitor pages you visit in the background, and we NEVER collect keystrokes, personal passwords, payment details, or any sensitive personal data.

3. Browser Permissions Usage (Disclosure)

To operate seamlessly, the TabMark Extension requests the following browser permissions for specific technical purposes:

  • activeTab & tabs: Used solely to read the URL and title of the current active tab when you click the extension popup to save it. It does not monitor background browsing.
  • storage: Used to cache your bookmark collections list and session state locally for fast offline access and instant responsiveness.
  • contextMenus: Adds a right-click context menu option ("Lưu vào TabMark") allowing you to save the current page or a selected link with one click.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to any third party or advertising brokers.

We work only with reputable cloud infrastructure providers to deliver our core service:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL Cloud): For encrypted database storage, authentication, and real-time syncing. Data is transferred over secure HTTPS/TLS.
  • Google Gemini AI (Optional): If you choose to use AI auto-categorization or summaries, only the bookmark text/summary is sent to the Gemini API. No personally identifiable information is sold or used for training unauthorized third-party models.
  • Vercel: For hosting the web dashboard application.

5. Data Retention, User Control & Deletion

  • Full User Control: You can edit, categorize, or permanently delete any saved bookmark or collection directly from your TabMark Dashboard at any time.
  • Account Deletion: If you wish to delete your account and all associated bookmarks permanently, you may request deletion via your account settings or by emailing our support.
